Prabhakar Kumar Singh

Researcher — Solid-State Additive Manufacturing

I develop solid-state additive manufacturing routes for lightweight alloys — Powder Bed Friction Stir (PBFS) deposition of magnesium and hybrid friction stir-based additive manufacturing of aluminum. My work spans process development, tool design and thermo-mechanical processing, linking processing to microstructure, mechanical performance and corrosion behaviour.

Research

Focus areas

Powder Bed Friction Stir (PBFS) of Mg alloys

A novel solid-state route for AZ31B deposition: tool feature and parameter selection, through-thickness property gradients, and substantially improved corrosion resistance over conventional routes.

AZ31BProcess developmentCorrosion

Hybrid friction stir additive manufacturing of Al alloys

Powder-based additive friction stir deposition on a vertical milling platform — friction-stirring pitch, feeding strategy and print-location effects on microstructure and microhardness.

AluminiumTool designHybrid AM

Characterisation & thermo-mechanical analysis

FE-SEM, EBSD, TEM, XCT, XRD, residual stress and corrosion analysis, combined with thermal-history monitoring and MATLAB-based XCT quantification of defects.
